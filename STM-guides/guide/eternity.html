⛏ Solo Event Guide

Eternity's Reach

Mine smarter. Move faster. Score higher.

Eternity's Reach is a 30-minute solo event where your goal is to collect as much Copper Ore as possible to climb the rankings and earn valuable rewards.

The secret to a high score is not staying on mines all game — it's combining hunting, mining, and smart movement.

📋 Before The Event

  • ✅ Register for your preferred time slot.
  • ✅ Have all marches available.
  • ✅ VIP 6 with a 5th march is a huge advantage.
  • ✅ Bring Teleports.
  • ✅ Activate March Speed and Gathering buffs.
  • ✅ Enter the map early and scout your surroundings.

🎯 First Priority

⚔ Do NOT Start Mining

During the first minutes of the event, focus on:

⚔ Hunting Level 2 Caesar Guards.

Your goal is to gain XP as quickly as possible and unlock your Talent Tree.

The sooner your talents are unlocked, the higher your final score will be.

⭐ Optimal Game Plan

Phase 1 — First 10 Minutes

⚔ Hunt only Level 2 Caesar Guards.

❌ Ignore regular mines.

Rush your RRLLR talent build.

Phase 2 — After Left 3 Unlock

Every 60 seconds:

➡️ Enter ONE regular mine.
💰 Collect the instant 5,000 Copper bonus.
➡️ Recall immediately.

Repeat throughout the match.

Phase 3 — Fractured Veins

🚨 Recall ALL marches.

⛏ Send every march to Fractured Veins.

⚡ Activate Right 5 gathering skill.

This is where the biggest Copper gains happen.

Phase 4 — Final Minutes

Return immediately to:

⚔ Caesar Guards
+
💰 5,000 Copper mine bonus every minute.

💡 Pro Tips

  • 🥇 Do not camp on regular mines.
  • 🥇 Spend most of the event hunting Caesar Guards.
  • 🥇 Never miss your 5,000 Copper bonus.
  • 🥇 Teleport if your area becomes overcrowded.
  • 🥇 Avoid fighting whales for Peak of Eternity unless necessary.
  • 🥇 Keep every march active at all times.
Idle marches are wasted points.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Mining from the very beginning.
  • ❌ Choosing the wrong talent path.
  • ❌ Forgetting the 5,000 Copper bonus.
  • ❌ Ignoring Fractured Veins.
  • ❌ Fighting over the center with whales.
  • ❌ Leaving marches idle.
