Strongest Governor

"Those who save, score. Those who spend everything early... watch everyone else climb the leaderboard." 😅

Strongest Governor is all about preparation. The players who plan ahead—not the ones who spend everything immediately—earn the best rewards.

📖 What is Strongest Governor?

Strongest Governor is a 7-day event where players earn points by completing different objectives each day.

The secret isn't playing more... it's using the right resources on the right day.

Even if you're not aiming for the top rankings, careful planning can still earn excellent milestone rewards.

📅 Day 1 – Development

Score Points

  • 🏗 Construction
  • 🔬 Research
  • 📈 Overall Power

Tips

  • Save long construction projects for today.
  • Use Research Speedups only during this stage.
  • Leave buildings almost finished and complete them after reset.

📅 Day 2 – Hero Growth

Score Points

  • 🦸 Hero Shards
  • 🏅 Hero Medals
  • ⭐ Hero XP

Tips

  • Upgrade Heroes today.
  • Avoid spending Hero Shards beforehand.
  • If you're preparing for KvK, think carefully before using premium resources.

📅 Day 3 – Troop Training

Score Points

  • 🪖 Training Troops

Tips

  • Keep Barracks training at all times.
  • Save Training Speedups.
  • Queue long trainings before reset and finish them today.

📅 Day 4 – Invader Hunt

Score Points

  • ⚔ Defeating Invaders

Tips

  • Save your Stamina.
  • Activate damage buffs.
  • Chain attacks together for maximum efficiency.

📅 Day 5 – Growth Day

Score Points

  • 🏗 Construction
  • 🔬 Research
  • 🦸 Hero Upgrades
  • 🪖 Troop Training

Tips

This is one of the best days to spend the resources you've been saving all week.

📅 Day 6 – Military Power

Score Points

  • 🪖 Training & Promoting Troops
  • ⚔ Military Upgrades

Tips

  • Promoting existing troops is usually more efficient than training from scratch.
  • Save Speedups for this stage.

📅 Day 7 – Elimination

Score Points

  • ⚔ Defeating Enemy Troops
Only participate if the rewards justify the losses.

Don't sacrifice thousands of troops just for a few extra ranking points.

📦 What Should You Save?

  • ✅ Speedups
  • ✅ Hero Shards
  • ✅ Hero Medals
  • ✅ Hero XP
  • ✅ Resources
  • ✅ Stamina
  • ✅ Long Research
  • ✅ Long Construction
  • ✅ Troops ready for Promotion

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Spending Hero Shards immediately.
  • ❌ Using Speedups randomly.
  • ❌ Upgrading Heroes every day.
  • ❌ Training troops without a plan.
  • ❌ Completing major research outside the event.

💎 Strategy

🆓 Free-to-Play

  • Focus on milestone rewards.
  • Don't compete with heavy spenders.
  • Consistency beats one lucky event.

💳 Spenders

  • Plan your purchases.
  • Buying and using packs on the correct day provides much greater value.

⭐ Special Tip – KvK is Almost Here!

KvK usually offers much better rewards than Strongest Governor.

Our recommendation:

  • ✅ Save your Hammers.
  • ✅ Save your True Gold.
  • ✅ Save your Hero Shards.
  • ✅ Save your Speedups whenever possible.
  • ✅ Don't waste valuable resources just to climb a few leaderboard positions.

Sometimes finishing slightly lower in Strongest Governor means entering KvK much stronger. 💪

🏆 Golden Rule

"Think of Strongest Governor as the warm-up... and KvK as the championship match."

Save wisely.
Spend strategically.
Prepare for the events that matter most.

😂 Gamer Reminder

Not every leaderboard is worth emptying your inventory. 😅

Save your Hammers, True Gold, Hero Shards, and Speedups for KvK.

Today's patience is tomorrow's MVP.

See you on the battlefield! ⚔️🔥
