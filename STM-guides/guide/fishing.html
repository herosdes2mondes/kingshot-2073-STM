Ultimate Fishing Guide

Fish deeper. Upgrade smarter. Score higher.

Learn how to maximize your Fishing Points, complete your Fish Collection, and climb the leaderboard using the most efficient upgrade strategy.

📅 Event Overview

Event Duration

August 4, 2026 (00:00 UTC)
→
August 6, 2026 (24:00 UTC)

During these three days you'll fish, upgrade your equipment, complete your Fish Collection and compete for leaderboard rewards.

🎣 How Fishing Works

  • 🎣 Start with 5 Fishing Baits.
  • ⏰ Gain 1 Bait every 3 hours.
  • 📦 Maximum storage: 10 Baits.
  • 🏆 Every fishing attempt rewards Fishing Points and Fishing Tokens.
Fishing Tokens are used to upgrade your equipment.

⬆️ Upgrade Priority

Priority Equipment Reason
🥇 Fishing Line Reach deeper waters and unlock rarer fish.
🥈 Hook Catch more fish during the ascent.
🥉 Sinker Starts deeper but offers the smallest early benefit.
Rush Fishing Line Lv.10 → Hook Lv.10 → Finish Sinker last.

🌊 Fishing Strategy

⬇️ During the Descent

  • Avoid hitting fish.
  • Travel along the sides.
  • Go as deep as possible.

⬆️ During the Ascent

  • 🐟 Rare Fish
  • 📦 Treasure Chests
  • 🧜 Distressed Mermaids
  • ⭐ High-Star Fish

🧜 Special Mechanics

🧜 Mermaids

Rescue only Distressed Mermaids. Regular Mermaids don't provide bonus rewards.

🏮 Lantern

Save Lanterns for dives deeper than 300m.

🔍 Ocean Scanner

Locate rare fish and complete your Fish Collection faster.

🛡 Reel Stabilizer

Prevents collisions while descending.

📈 Event Progression

🌊 Ocean Prospector

  • ⭐ All gear becomes Lv.10.
  • 🐟 More rare fish.
  • 💎 Better rewards.
  • 🗺 Never miss the FREE daily map.

🐠 Fish Collection

Completing your collection unlocks permanent rewards such as Skins, Frames and Nameplates.

💰 Fishing Tokens

Spend every Token upgrading your gear until everything reaches Lv.10. After that, invest them in the Sunken Treasure Hunt.

⚡ Power Cast

Best used together with Ocean Prospector and Fishing Vouchers.

💡 Pro Tips

  • 🎣 Descend along the sides.
  • 🐟 Prioritize rare fish.
  • 🌊 Use Ocean Prospector every day.
  • 🗺 Check your Fish Collection before every run.
  • 🎫 Save Vouchers for your highest-value runs.
  • 📦 Buy the Fishing Pass on Day 1 if you plan to spend.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Upgrading the Sinker first.
  • ❌ Fishing only near the surface.
  • ❌ Forgetting the free Ocean Prospector map.
  • ❌ Wasting Lanterns on shallow dives.
  • ❌ Ignoring your Fish Collection.
  • ❌ Spending Tokens before maxing your gear.
  • ❌ Using Vouchers on low-value runs.
  • ❌ Hitting fish while descending.

🏆 Quick Summary

✅ Max Fishing Line first.

✅ Hook second.

✅ Sinker last.

✅ Fish as deep as possible.

✅ Use Ocean Prospector daily.

✅ Complete your Fish Collection.

✅ Max gear before using Tokens in Sunken Treasure Hunt.

✅ Save special items if you're not pushing leaderboard rankings.

🎣 Golden Rule

"Fish smarter—not just more."

Depth beats quantity. Planning beats luck. Upgrade wisely, complete your collection, and save your best items for your biggest runs.

Good luck, Chiefs! 🎣🐟🏆
