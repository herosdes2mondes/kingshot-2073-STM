PvE Events

Viking Vengeance

Survive the waves. Protect your Alliance. Maximize your score.

Viking Vengeance is a fully cooperative event where teamwork always beats individual strength. Help your Alliance survive all 20 waves and earn the highest rewards together.

📋 Before the Event

  • ✅ Stay online throughout the event whenever possible.
  • ✅ Keep your 3 strongest Heroes defending your city.
  • ✅ Do NOT heal troops during the event.
  • ✅ Coordinate beforehand who will reinforce teammates and who will defend the Headquarters.

🛡 Troop Setup

📤 Send as Reinforcements

  • 🛡 All Infantry
  • 🐎 All Cavalry

🏠 Keep at Home

🏹 Keep your Archers defending your city.

This is the preferred strategy for most Alliances since Archers provide excellent defensive damage while your Infantry and Cavalry reinforce teammates.

🌊 Event Waves

Wave Target
1 – 6 🏘 All Cities
7 ⚠ Online Players Only
8 – 9 🏘 All Cities
10 🏰 Alliance Headquarters
11 – 13 🏘 All Cities
14 ⚠ Online Players Only
15 – 16 🏘 All Cities
17 ⚠ Online Players Only
18 – 19 🏘 All Cities
20 🏰 Final Headquarters Attack

🏰 Defending the Headquarters

  • ✅ As soon as Wave 9 ends, recall one march.
  • 🏰 Immediately reinforce the Alliance Headquarters.
  • 🤝 Once Wave 10 ends, return to reinforcing teammates.
  • 🔁 Repeat the exact same process before Wave 20.
Missing HQ defense can cost your entire Alliance valuable points.

🚑 Don't Heal Troops

One of the most common mistakes during Viking Vengeance is healing troops.

  • ❌ Don't waste Speedups.
  • ❌ Don't spend Resources.

Remember

All defeated troops automatically return when the event ends.

Save your resources for events where losses actually matter.

⭐ Viking Secrets

🥇 Reinforcing earns more points

Reinforcing teammates generally provides more value than only defending your own city.

🤝 Prioritize Online Players

Active players can defend themselves, reinforce others, defend the HQ, and reposition troops quickly.

📍 Never Leave Troops Idle

Finished defending?
Immediately reinforce another teammate.

🏰 HQ Comes First

Waves 10 and 20 are Headquarters-only attacks. Never miss them.

🔥 Keep Fighting

Even if your city burns, continue reinforcing teammates and defending the HQ. You can still earn valuable points.

💬 Communication Wins Battles

Use Alliance Chat or Discord to coordinate reinforcements, HQ defense, and players who still need support.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Healing troops during the event.
  • ❌ Forgetting HQ defense.
  • ❌ Leaving troops idle.
  • ❌ Reinforcing full cities while others have no support.
  • ❌ Quitting after your city gets defeated.

🏆 Quick Summary

✅ Stay online.

✅ Keep your strongest Heroes at home.

✅ Reinforce teammates with Infantry & Cavalry.

✅ Leave Archers defending your city.

✅ Defend the HQ during Waves 10 & 20.

✅ Never heal troops.

✅ Never leave troops idle.

✅ Continue helping even if your city is defeated.

⚔ Golden Rule

"Viking Vengeance isn't about proving who's the strongest player... It's about proving which Alliance works best as a team."

The more you help your teammates, the higher your score, the better your rewards, and the greater your Alliance's chances of victory.

Teamwork always defeats individual power. 🛡️🔥
