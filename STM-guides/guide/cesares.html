Cesares Fury

Smart progression beats raw power.

Push your limits every month, unlock higher difficulties, and maximize rewards for both yourself and your Alliance.

🎯 Main Objectives

  • ✅ Clear all 50 stages whenever possible.
  • ✅ Unlock the highest difficulty available for next month's event.
  • ✅ Always challenge the highest unlocked difficulty for better rewards.
Progress made this month makes next month's event much easier and more rewarding.

📋 Before You Start

  • ⚔ Attempt every stage Solo first.
  • 🔄 If you fail, your Stamina is refunded—there's no risk.
  • 🦸 Use Diana whenever possible to reduce Stamina consumption.
  • 📜 Save your Cesares Accords for difficult stages.

📈 Smart Progression

Difficulty increases significantly at the following milestones:

Stage Recommendation
10 ⭐ Prepare stronger marches.
20 ⭐ Save additional Accords.
30 ⭐⭐ Increase march quality.
40 ⭐⭐ Save your strongest teams.
50 🏆 Use every available advantage.
Don't waste your strongest marches on easy stages. Save them for the milestone fights.

🤝 Alliance Tips

  • 🤝 Help 5 Alliance members every day.
  • 📜 Earn up to 10 Cesares Accords.
  • ⚙ Enable Auto Rally to help teammates more efficiently.
  • 💪 A stronger Alliance benefits everyone.

👑 Captain Secret

🎁 Easy Rewards

The Captain only requires one successful hit to qualify for rewards.

  • ✅ Send 1 troop with NO Heroes.
  • ✅ Even dealing 0.0% damage still counts.
  • ✅ Wait until every Alliance member has landed a hit before defeating the Captain.
A few seconds of patience can guarantee full rewards for your entire Alliance.

💡 Pro Tips

  • ⭐ Always push your highest unlocked difficulty.
  • ⭐ Don't spend all your Accords on easy stages.
  • ⭐ Failed Solo attempts are free—use them to test your limits.
  • ⭐ Coordinate Captain timing with your Alliance.
  • ⭐ Every month's progress improves next month's rewards.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Spending all Accords on easy stages.
  • ❌ Skipping Solo attempts before requesting help.
  • ❌ Forgetting to help Alliance members.
  • ❌ Defeating the Captain before everyone lands a hit.
  • ❌ Not pushing the highest unlocked difficulty.

🏆 Quick Summary

✅ Clear all 50 stages if possible.

✅ Attempt every stage Solo first.

✅ Use Diana to save Stamina.

✅ Save Accords for milestone stages.

✅ Help 5 Alliance members every day.

✅ Wait for everyone to hit the Captain before finishing it.

⚔ Golden Rule

"Progress today means better rewards every month."

Fight smart.
Help your teammates.
Push your highest difficulty.

Stronger players build a stronger Alliance—and a stronger Alliance helps everyone reach even greater heights. ⚔️🏆
