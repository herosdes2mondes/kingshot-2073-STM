Progression & Account Growth

Ultimate Island Guide

Build smarter. Grow faster. Unlock your Island's full potential.

Your Island is much more than decoration—it's a permanent source of economic growth, military bonuses, and quality-of-life improvements.

🌊 First Priorities

⛲ Fountain of Life

Upgrade it to the highest level possible. Your Fountain generates the Water used for nearly every Island upgrade.

💧 Reservoirs

Upgrade your Reservoirs whenever available. More Water production means faster Island progression.

Never let your Fountain or Reservoirs fall behind. They are the foundation of every future upgrade.

🌵 Clear Your Island

Use Water from your Reservoirs to remove every Cactus blocking your Island.

  • 📦 Earn reward chests for every cleared area.
  • 🌴 Unlock new building space.
  • ✨ Receive the Purifier after clearing the entire Island.
The Purifier increases your workers' Water production, making future upgrades much faster.

🏛 Building Priority

Priority Focus Reason
🥇 March Capacity Stronger marches in every event.
🥈 March Buffs Permanent combat bonuses.
🥉 Healing Speed Recover troops much faster.

🌱 Free-to-Play Progression

If you're a Free-to-Play or low-spending player, prioritize upgrading every Blue Growth Building to Level 5.

Recommended Upgrade Order

  1. Research Speed
  2. Construction Speed
  3. Troop Training Speed
  4. Gathering Speed

⚔ Military Buildings

Most F2P players spend more time joining rallies than leading them, so focus on efficient military upgrades.

⚓ First Upgrade

Upgrade the Basic Dock first. Increasing Deployment Capacity provides value in almost every Alliance event.

Recommended Troop Order

  • 🥇 Infantry
  • 🥈 Marksmen
  • 🥉 Cavalry

🌅 Lighthouse & Wonders

Once you unlock the Lighthouse, start collecting General Wonder Blueprints.

Blueprint Conversion

10 General Wonder Blueprints

➜

1 Golden Sunset Wonder Blueprint

Save your Blueprints until you can craft the stronger version.

🤝 Help Your Alliance

Visit your Alliance members' Islands every day.

  • 💧 Help refill their Fountain.
  • 👍 Like their Island.
  • 🎁 Earn extra Water for helping.
Helping your teammates benefits everyone—including you.

💡 Pro Tips

  • ⛲ Upgrade Fountain and Reservoirs every chance you get.
  • 🌵 Clear every Cactus as early as possible.
  • 🏛 Prioritize permanent account bonuses.
  • 🌱 Upgrade all Blue Growth Buildings to Lv.5 (F2P).
  • ⚓ Upgrade the Basic Dock first.
  • 🛡 Focus Infantry → Marksmen → Cavalry.
  • 🤝 Visit Alliance Islands every day.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Ignoring the Fountain of Life.
  • ❌ Leaving Cactus uncleared.
  • ❌ Prioritizing decorative buildings.
  • ❌ Spreading military upgrades equally.
  • ❌ Forgetting to help Alliance members.
  • ❌ Spending Wonder Blueprints too early.

✅ Unlock the Purifier.

🏝 Golden Rule

"Your Island isn't built in a day—but every upgrade lasts forever."

Prioritize permanent growth over short-term gains, help your Alliance daily, and your Island will become one of your strongest long-term investments.
