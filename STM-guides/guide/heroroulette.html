Hero Roulette

Hero Roulette is the best place to spend your Gems.

But spinning without a plan is just wasting resources. Save smart, spin wisely, and maximize your Hero progression.

🎯 Your Goal

Spins Recommendation
120 🏆 Best value and maximum rewards.
70 ✅ Good stopping point if you can't reach 120.
Below 70 🚫 Save your Gems for the next Hero Roulette.

💎 Save Your Gems

  • 💰 Don't waste Gems on random events.
  • ⭐ Prioritize the Hero Roulette that provides the biggest upgrade for your account.
  • 📅 Plan ahead instead of spending impulsively.
Every Gem spent outside your plan delays your next Hero upgrade.

🎟 Free Tokens

  • ✅ Claim your FREE Roulette Token every day.
  • ✅ Tokens carry over between Hero Roulettes.
  • ✅ Save them for the Hero you really want.

🦸 Hero Priority

Generation Hero Recommendation
Gen 1 Saul ⚠ Usually not worth heavy investment.
Gen 2 Zoe ⭐⭐⭐ One of the best investments.
Gen 3 Petra ⭐⭐⭐ Excellent long-term value.
Later Generations Current Featured Hero Choose the Hero that best fits your lineup.

💡 Pro Tip

Build One Hero at a Time

Once you've unlocked the Roulette Hero, continue upgrading them using Universal Mythic Hero Shards.

Focus on completing one Hero before investing heavily in another.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Spending Gems on every Hero Roulette.
  • ❌ Spinning beyond 120 Spins.
  • ❌ Upgrading multiple Heroes simultaneously.
  • ❌ Ignoring free daily Roulette Tokens.

🏆 Quick Summary

✅ Aim for 120 Spins.

✅ Settle for 70 if necessary.

✅ Save Gems if you can't reach 70.

✅ Claim your FREE Token every day.

✅ Focus on one Hero at a time.

✅ Prioritize the Heroes that fit your lineup.

🎰 Golden Rule

"Smart planning beats lucky spins."

Save your Gems.
Spin at the right time.
Invest in the right Hero.

A well-planned Roulette will always provide more value than chasing every new Hero. 💎🎰
