Pets Guide

Pets aren't just for looks — they are a HUGE source of power. Every pet increases your account strength with combat bonuses, and each one provides a unique skill that can improve your progression. Choose your pets wisely and invest your resources strategically.

🦴 How Pets Work

Every Pet contributes to your account progression by providing combat bonuses and unique abilities.

Every Pet Provides:

  • ⚔️ Squad ATK & DEF
    Increased as your Pet Level grows.
  • ❤️ Health & ☠️ Lethality
    Unlocked at Level 10 through Refinement.
  • ⭐ Unique Pet Skill
    Unlocked at Level 10 and improved through advancement (feeding).

⬆️ Upgrade Priority

Focus your resources on the pets that provide the highest value for your current stage of progression.

Pet Main Benefit Priority
🐺 Gray Wolf Construction Speed Early Game
🦬 Bison Instant Gathering Economy
🐆 Cheetah More Pet Food Growth
🦁 Lion Excellent long-term value through free resources. Long Term
🐻 Combat Pets Improve according to your troop composition. Combat

🎯 Daily Routine

  • ✅ Claim Pet rewards every day.
  • ✅ Activate Pet Skills whenever they are available.
  • ✅ Never waste stamina or Pet Skill cooldowns.

🔥 Refinement Tips

  • 💙 Use Common Taming Marks on lower-rarity Pets.
  • 💜 Save Advanced Taming Marks for stronger Pets (Blue+ refinement or later generations).
  • 🎯 Focus on refining one important Pet at a time.

🚫 Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Ignoring Pet Skills.
  • ❌ Refining every Pet at the same time.
  • ❌ Wasting Advanced Taming Marks too early.
  • ❌ Forgetting to level Pets and unlock their skills.

🏆 Final Advice

"A strong Pet lineup means stronger marches, faster growth, and a big advantage in every event."

Build smart, refine wisely, and let your Pets do the heavy lifting! 🐾🔥
