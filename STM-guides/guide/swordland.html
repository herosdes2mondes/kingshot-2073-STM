Alliance PvP Guide

Swordland Showdown

Swordland is won by teamwork — not individual power.

The Alliance that communicates, controls objectives, and fights together will always have the advantage.

📝 Before The Battle

  • ✅ Register for the correct Legion and time slot.
  • ⏰ Be online 10–15 minutes before the event starts.
  • 🚀 Activate all available buffs (Pets, City Buffs, etc.).
  • 📢 Join Discord or Voice Chat if your Alliance uses it.
  • 📦 Recall all marches before entering the battlefield.

🎯 Your Main Goal

Winning Isn't Everything

🏆 Winning is great...

💎 Maximizing your Personal Relic Points is even more important.

Your final rewards are determined by:

  • 🛡 Alliance Result
  • ⭐ Personal Relic Points
Even if your Alliance loses, a high personal score can still earn excellent rewards.

🗺 Building Priority

Priority Building Benefit
1️⃣ 🐎 Royal Stables Reduces Teleport Cooldown.
2️⃣ 🔔 Bell Tower Faster building occupation.
3️⃣ ⚔ Sword Shrine Main objective once unlocked.
4️⃣ 🏰 Other Buildings Capture according to Rally Leader instructions.

⚔ During The Battle

  • 🎯 Stay with your team.
  • 🚫 Don't chase random enemies across the map.
  • 🏰 Capturing and defending buildings scores more than unnecessary fighting.
  • ⚡ Use your free teleports wisely to reinforce key objectives.

👥 Know Your Role

🛡 Tank

Hold buildings and absorb enemy attacks.

⚔ DPS

Join rallies and eliminate enemy reinforcements.

❤️ Support

Reinforce allies, join rallies quickly, and help defend important objectives.

Every role matters. Victory comes from teamwork—not individual statistics.

💎 Pro Tips

  • ⭐ Capture buildings early for the First Occupation bonus.
  • 💰 Collect dropped Relic Loot whenever possible.
  • 📍 Stay close to your Alliance.
  • 🚀 Use free March Speedups whenever available.
  • ♻️ If completely wiped out early, leave the battlefield and return after the cooldown with restored troops if enough time remains.

🚫 Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Fighting in the middle of nowhere.
  • ❌ Ignoring important buildings.
  • ❌ Teleporting alone into enemy territory.
  • ❌ Forgetting to collect Relic Loot.
  • ❌ Ignoring R4/R5 instructions.
  • ❌ Playing solo instead of supporting the Alliance.

🏆 Quick Summary

✅ Arrive early and activate buffs.

✅ Prioritize Royal Stables → Bell Tower → Sword Shrine.

✅ Stay with your Alliance.

✅ Capture and defend objectives.

✅ Collect Relic Loot.

✅ Follow your Rally Leader's calls.

⚔ Golden Rule

"Swordland isn't about getting the most kills... It's about controlling objectives, earning Relic Points, and fighting as ONE TEAM."

Stick together.
Follow the calls.
Trust your leaders.

Victory will follow. 🔥

😂 Alliance Rule

If you ignore the Rally Leader and decide to become the "main character"...

...don't worry.

The enemy Alliance will happily remind you that Swordland is a team event. 🤣⚔️
