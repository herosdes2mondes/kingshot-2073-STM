Castle Battle

Capture the Castle. Control the Towers. Stay in the fight from beginning to end.

Castle Battle rewards players who remain active throughout the entire event. Healing, rejoining battles, and supporting your Alliance are the keys to both victory and high personal scores.

🎯 Main Goal

Control the Castle and the surrounding Towers while maximizing your personal contribution.

🏆 If you want a high score, don't stop fighting. Every return to the battlefield is another opportunity to earn points.

🏆 How to Score Big

You earn Personal Points by:

  • ⚔ Defeating enemy troops.
  • 🏰 Occupying the Castle and Towers.
  • ❤️ Healing wounded troops and returning to battle.

🔥 Top Player Secret

The highest-ranking players never stop fighting.

Fight → Heal → Rejoin → Repeat.

❤️ Heal Smart

  • ✅ Empty your Infirmary before the event.
  • ✅ Stock plenty of Resources.
  • ✅ Use Batch Heal whenever possible.
  • ✅ Request Alliance Help.
  • ✅ Jump back into battle immediately.
❗ Never allow your Infirmary to become full. Once it's full, wounded troops become permanent losses.

⚔ Recommended Troop Formations

Situation Formation
⚔ Rally Attack 🛡 50% Infantry • 🐎 20% Lancers • 🏹 30% Marksmen
🏰 Castle / Tower Defense 🛡 60% Infantry • 🐎 40% Lancers • 🏹 0% Marksmen
🛡 Counter Infantry Garrisons 🛡 50% Infantry • 🏹 50% Marksmen • ❌ No Lancers
Adjust your troop composition depending on your objective instead of using the same march every time.

🦸 Hero Selection

👑 Rally Leaders

Use your strongest Expedition Attack heroes.

🤝 Rally Joiners

Don't send random heroes. Your Expedition skills help the entire Rally.

🏰 Castle & Tower Defense

Switch to your best Garrison heroes before reinforcing.

🏰 Don't Ignore the Towers

Towers are just as important as the Castle.

  • 🏰 They weaken enemy control.
  • ⚔ They make capturing the Castle much easier.
  • 📢 Reinforce immediately whenever your R4/R5 requests it.

💎 Pro Tips

  • ⭐ Heal constantly.
  • ⭐ Stay active for the entire event.
  • ⭐ Join every Rally you can.
  • ⭐ Reinforce Castle & Towers whenever called.
  • ⭐ Save Speedups for emergencies.
  • ⭐ The longer you fight, the higher your score.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Ignoring Tower control.
  • ❌ Forgetting to heal troops.
  • ❌ Letting the Infirmary become full.
  • ❌ Remaining idle after losing a march.
  • ❌ Using the same formation in every battle.
  • ❌ Ignoring Rally Leader instructions.

🏆 Quick Summary

✅ Capture the Castle and Towers.

✅ Heal constantly.

✅ Keep your Infirmary under control.

✅ Join every Rally.

✅ Reinforce objectives when called.

✅ Stay active from start to finish.

👑 Golden Rule

"The highest Castle Battle scores don't come from one massive fight... they come from fighting nonstop."

⚔ Fight.
❤️ Heal.
🏰 Reinforce.
🔁 Repeat.

Victory belongs to the Alliance that never stops pushing forward. 🔥🏰
