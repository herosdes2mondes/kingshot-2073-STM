Bear Hunt Guide

Bear Hunt is one of the most important Alliance events for earning Hammers, Essence Stones, and Gear Materials. Your goal is not to deal the highest personal damage — your goal is to maximize the Alliance's total damage.

🏹 Before The Event

  • ✅ Activate all available buffs (Pets, City Buffs, etc.).
  • ✅ Save your Bear Hunt formation in advance.
  • ✅ Teleport near the Pitfall if requested.
  • ✅ Make sure the Pitfall reaches Level 5 (+25% ATK) before the first rallies begin.
  • ❌ Do not waste Arrows after the Pitfall is maxed.

👑 If You Are A Rally Leader

Rally Leader Priority

  • 🔥 Use your 3 strongest Heroes.
  • ⚔ The Rally Leader benefits from all three Heroes' skills.
  • 🚀 Start a new Rally immediately after the previous one ends.
  • ⏱ Less downtime means more total damage.

🤝 If You Are Joining A Rally

⭐ Most Important Rule

Only the first Hero in your march provides a Rally Skill.

Also remember:
⚠️ Only 4 Joiner Skills are applied to each Rally.

A wrong Hero can replace a much better buff and reduce the entire Rally's damage.

🥇 Best Joiner Heroes

  • ✅ Chenko
  • ✅ Yeonwoo
  • ✅ Amane
  • ✅ Amadeus

❌ Don't Have Them?

Send your march WITHOUT A HERO.

Yes — No Hero is better than the wrong Hero.

🏹 Troop Composition

The Bear never attacks back. Therefore: Survivability does not matter.

Troop Recommended Reason
🛡 Infantry 10% Minimal requirement.
🐎 Cavalry 10% Minimal requirement.
🏹 Archers 80% Highest Bear Hunt damage.
🔥 Late Game 5 / 5 / 90 Maximum Archer focus.
If you cannot reach these ratios, simply send as many Archers as possible.

🎯 During The Event

  • ✔️ Re-send your march immediately.
  • ✔️ Join the strongest Rally Leaders whenever possible.
  • ✔️ Never let your march sit idle.

💎 Pro Tips

  • ⭐ Strongest players should launch the first rallies.
  • ⭐ Coordinate rallies to keep the Bear under constant attack.
  • ⭐ Use Formation Presets to join faster.
  • ⭐ Near the end of the event, joining an existing Rally is usually better than starting a new one.

🚫 Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Sending the wrong Hero.
  • ❌ Joining with defensive Heroes.
  • ❌ Ignoring Archer-heavy troop ratios.
  • ❌ Letting marches sit idle.
  • ❌ Forgetting buffs.
  • ❌ Forgetting to upgrade the Pitfall.
  • ❌ Wasting Arrows after Pitfall reaches Level 5.

🏆 Golden Rule

👑 Rally Leaders

➡️ Use your best Heroes.
➡️ Launch rallies non-stop.

🤝 Rally Joiners

➡️ Use Chenko / Yeonwoo / Amane / Amadeus / Margot.

➡️ Don't have them?
👉 Send NO HERO.

➡️ Fill your march.
➡️ Bring as many Archers as possible.
➡️ Always join the strongest rallies.

🔥 Remember

Bear Hunt is a TEAM event.

One wrong Hero can reduce the damage of the entire Rally.

Play for your Alliance — not only for your personal score! 🐻🏹🔥
